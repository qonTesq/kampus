# kampus

**Your campus, inside the AI assistant you already use.**

## The problem

Lots of students already ask ChatGPT or Claude for help, but those assistants know nothing about their campus. Deadlines are in Canvas, study rooms are on the library site, menus are on the dining site, and the shuttle has its own app. Simple questions mean juggling five apps and logins.

## The idea

kampus is a free, open-source connector that plugs your university into the AI assistant you already use. You log in once with your school account, then just ask:

- *"What's due this week?"*
- *"Book me a study room at 3."*
- *"Where can I eat near my next class?"*
- *"When's the next shuttle?"*

It works with ChatGPT, Claude and other assistants, typed or spoken.

## Why it's safe, and why schools would say yes

- **The school stays in control.** University IT decides which data each assistant and each role (student, faculty, staff, visitor) can see. Sensitive data like grades stays off unless the school approves it.
- **It doesn't keep your data.** It records which feature was used and when, never what you asked or the answer.
- **It's free and open source,** and schools can run it on their own servers.
- **It works at any university.** Adding a school means filling in one config file. Anyone can add a new feature.

## What we're building first

The first version starts with Temple and Saint Joseph's, to show the same software works at more than one school. There's also a 3D-printed talking version of your school's mascot that you can ask questions out loud.
